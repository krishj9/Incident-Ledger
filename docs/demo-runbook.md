# Demo Runbook

## Preflight
- Confirm demo environment has one enabled center and seed verification passes.
- Confirm iPhone and iPad are registered and online initially.
- Confirm mail-capture inbox is accessible.
- Confirm worker queue, notification delivery, and audit export health are green.

## Script
1. Sign in on iPhone as `DEMO-Alex Kim`; point out active user/role.
2. Start multi-child injury on iPad, choose primary affected child, complete facts, capture permitted image.
3. Open writing assistance; reject one suggestion and edit/accept another; show staff retains control.
4. Disable network; save and submit. Show exact pending-sync status.
5. Restore network; sync; show server receipt, director queue, and audit record.
6. Open seeded High/Critical report; show immediate alert and 30/60 minute escalation state.
7. Sign in as director; compare source and edited narrative, make neutral wording edit with reason, approve and show frozen version.
8. Complete in-person acknowledgement for one child packet.
9. Use synthetic email link for another child; demonstrate resend invalidation and disagreement.
10. Record/show three contact attempts across two dates; director closes unreachable.
11. Open restricted fixture; show structured observation, no AI, compliance routing, and guardian notification block.
12. Export audit packet; validate versions, evidence manifest, approval history, acknowledgement outcome, and export event.

## Failure handling
If a sync/notification task is delayed, use operations health page and correlation ID. Do not manually change database records to make a demo pass; reset synthetic fixture, record defect, and rerun the scripted path.
